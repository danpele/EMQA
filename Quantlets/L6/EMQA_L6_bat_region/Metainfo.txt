Name of QuantLet: 'EMQA_L6_bat_region'

Published in: 'Energy Markets Quantitative Analysis (EMQA)'

Description: 'Regional battery arbitrage: mean daily revenue (EUR per MW) of a 2h, 1 MW battery with perfect foresight on the day-ahead prices of Romania, Bulgaria and Hungary, monthly, 2024-01 to 2026-10, and the monthly mean output of the Romanian and Bulgarian battery fleets as reported to ENTSO-E. Prints the revenue of the last 12 months by zone (with Germany-Luxembourg) and the correlation of daily revenues between Romania and its neighbours.'

Keywords: 'battery, energy storage, arbitrage, regional market, market coupling, correlation, Romania, Bulgaria, Hungary, Germany, ENTSO-E'
