Typical errors in energy data: a wrong formula; look-ahead bias (using load, generation or prices
that are not yet known at gate closure, 12:00 CET on day d-1); CET (Central European Time) mixed up with
UTC (Coordinated Universal Time) or with Romanian local time; daylight-saving days with 23 or 25 hours;
hourly and 15-minute prices mixed after 1 October 2025; a sign error (for example VaR, Value-at-Risk,
or a cash flow reported with the wrong sign); a log transform applied to negative prices;
data or numbers that were invented; a reference that does not exist.
